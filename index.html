Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions from parents and students about coaching at Lakshya Private Tuitions

What courses does Lakshya Private Tuitions offer?
Lakshya Private Tuitions offers Class 8–10 foundation tuition, Class 11–12 Science and Commerce coaching (Maharashtra State Board), and competitive exam preparation for JEE Mains & Advanced, NEET and MHT-CET. The Mulund West branch specialises in Science and entrance exams, while the Dombivli East branch offers both Science and Commerce streams along with Class 8–10.
Where is Lakshya Private Tuitions located?
Lakshya Private Tuitions has two branches: Mulund West — Floor 2, Keshav Bhavan, MG Road, beside Mensa Classes, near Shiv Sena Shakha, Mulund West, Mumbai 400080 (Phone: +91 91366 55448). Dombivli East — 310, Navare Plaza, opposite Ram Nagar Police Station, Ramnagar, Dombivli East 421201 (Phone: +91 91677 88777).
What is the batch size at Lakshya Private Tuitions?
Batches are limited to a maximum of 20 students. This ensures every student receives personalised attention and the faculty can address individual doubts promptly.
Does Lakshya offer a free demo class?
Yes. Students can attend a free demo class at either branch before enrolling. Call or WhatsApp the respective branch number to schedule your demo: Mulund West +91 91366 55448, Dombivli East +91 91677 88777.
What are the class timings?
Classes run Monday to Saturday from 7:00 AM to 9:00 PM. Morning, afternoon and evening batches are available to accommodate different school schedules. Sunday sessions are available by appointment.
Is JEE coaching available in Mulund West?
Yes. Lakshya Private Tuitions offers JEE Mains and Advanced preparation at the Mulund West branch covering Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics with weekly mock tests, DPP sheets and one-on-one doubt sessions.
Does Lakshya offer NEET coaching in Mulund?
Yes. NEET coaching is available at the Mulund West branch with NCERT-focused teaching for Physics, Chemistry and Biology. Regular mock tests and previous year paper practice help students target government medical colleges.
